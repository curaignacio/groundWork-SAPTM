Transportation Management (TM)

## Use

Transportation Management (TM) supports you in all activities connected with the physical transportation of goods from one

location to another. You can use TM to perform the following activities, for example:

Work with TM-relevant orders and deliveries

Create forwarding orders for your ordering parties

Create freight units

## Create delivery proposals

Create freight orders and freight bookings (manually or automatically using transportation planning)

Perform carrier selection

## Create agreements

Tender transportation services

## Dispatch and monitor the transportation

Calculate the transportation charges for both the ordering party and the supplier side

Consider foreign trade and dangerous goods regulations

## Use integrated analytics functions and KPIs for real-time performance visibility

You can use TM to create and monitor an efficient transportation plan that fulfills the relevant constraints (for example, service

level, costs, and resource availability). You can determine options to save costs and to optimize the use of available resources. You

can react to transportation events and find solutions to possible deviations from the original transportation plan.

Creating an efficient transportation plan and optimizing the use of available resources also enables you to reduce greenhouse gas

(GHG) emissions. TM also allows you to specify GHG emissions for your trucks and take them into account during planning. For

more information, see Sustainability.

## SAP Global Trade Services

You can perform customs processing for your TM business

documents in a connected global trade services (GTS) system. For

example, you can request export declarations for freight orders or

freight bookings. The data is transferred using enterprise services.

## Document Information Extraction

You can use this service to extract data from delivery documents

attached to a consignment order. For more information, see AI-

Assisted Goods Receipt Analysis.

Note also the following:

You use PS&S Services in TM to ensure the safe transportation of dangerous goods in accordance with legal regulations

(see Considering Dangerous Goods).

You can integrate TM with external systems by using enterprise services. For example, you can automatically create and

confirm forwarding orders based on data received from your ordering parties.
