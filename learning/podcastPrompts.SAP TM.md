SESSION NUMBER AND TITLE: Session 03 — Locations, Zones, Trade Lanes, Modes and Resources

AUDIENCE: SAP TM consultant in training

KNOWLEDGE TO ACQUIRE: Transportation network building blocks: locations, transportation zones, transportation lanes, transshipment locations, default routes, schedules and trade lanes; the Transportation Network Cockpit displays locations, zones, lanes, trade lanes, schedules, default routes, connections and business partner networks on a map; planning algorithms use the network to find the most efficient path | Locations: a logical or physical place where products or resources are managed on a quantity basis; every freight unit has a source and destination location; location types (for example 1001 Production Plant, 1002 Distribution Center, 1003 Shipping Point, 1021 Business Partner, 1100 Port, 1110 Airport) are only a reference and do not control business processes; the address of a shipping point is represented as a location | Creating locations: on the fly from sales orders, purchase orders, stock transfer orders and deliveries; with report /SAPAPO/CREATE_LOCATION for plants, shipping points and business partners; with BAdI /SAPAPO/LOC_CREATE when a business partner is saved; one-time locations are transient and used for example in forwarding orders; a business partner can have several locations, one per address | Geocoding: geo-coordinates (longitude, latitude, altitude) are derived from the address; standard SAP applications determine them at region level, which suffices unless distances, durations or map display are needed; the Precision field shows the granularity; two geocoding levels exist, standard (in the license) and full addresses (depends on the geocoding software) | Transshipment locations: used to unload goods from one freight order or vehicle resource and load them onto another, for different means of transport or carriers (for example truck-ocean-truck) or for consolidation and deconsolidation; defined by assigning them to a location or transportation zone | Transportation zones: group locations (including one-time locations and subzones) so that zone properties pass to the locations and less master data is needed; they have no address but can have coordinates, either calculated as the center point of the included locations or entered manually; types are region, postal code, direct and mixed | Transportation zone hierarchy: properties of higher-level zones pass to lower levels; a zone cannot be assigned to itself or to a zone it already contains; a zone can be in any number of higher-level zones and a location in any number of zones; the VSR optimizer, lane determination and transshipment location determination consider the hierarchy | Transportation lanes: express direct reachability between two locations, two zones or a mix for a specific means of transport; a lane points in one direction only, so the reverse needs another lane; at least one means of transport must be assigned, carriers can be assigned per means of transport (with cost, priority, share of business or capacity), and carriers are needed for automatic carrier selection; created in the Define Transportation Lane app | Trade lanes: abstract, context-independent classification of transport with an orientation (Inbound, Outbound, From, To, Within, Along), source and destination, optionally a mode and means of transport; they form hierarchies and can overlap; they do not replace transportation lanes and have no direct link to them | Transportation mode and means of transport: the mode is the highest-level grouping (categories road, rail, sea, air, postal service, inland waterways); the main carriage mode holds the shipment documents in multistage shipments; a means of transport describes a vehicle type and carries mode, multi-resource, scheduled, own means of transport and GIS quality settings; UN/EDIFACT standard codes include 006 Aircraft, 011 Ship, 031 Truck, 038 Car; without a GIS, average speed and distance factor estimate transit time | Multi-resource: a means of transport can be flagged so that the system assumes a defined number of resources (unlimited if none given) instead of individual resources; the number is the maximum of parallel freight orders per resource and it can model an external fleet in one step | Path finder: searches legs in the network over locations, transshipment locations, transportation lanes, schedules and zones to show whether a connection exists and master data is correct; it runs in the transportation network cockpit (Find Path To Location) or on the transportation proposals UI, and as report /SCMTMS/FIND_TN_PATH; it does not consider incompatibilities | Resource types: vehicle, handling, calendar and transportation unit resource (H03 also lists driver); a vehicle resource is a unit load with up to eight capacity dimensions that planning only uses if they match a freight unit building rule, and can be active or passive; Factory Calendar, Continuous Dimension, Capacity and Unit of Measure are mandatory | Other resource objects: compartments divide a vehicle resource and support incompatibilities (for example dangerous goods areas); means-of-transport combinations model truck and trailer; handling resources (door, loading ramp, forklift) give loading and unloading capacity; calendar resources set location operating hours; transportation unit resources represent containers; resource classes are determined from the means of transport or equipment group | Incompatibilities for resources in the exercise: incompatibilities define objects that must not be combined, for example ice cream and vehicles without a cooling unit; INC_S4TM1_VEH_FU links freight units to vehicle resources/types, and violations can be allowed or forbidden separately for manual planning and VSR optimization

SESSION NUMBER AND TITLE: Session 05 — Logistics Integration with Sales, Purchasing and Deliveries

AUDIENCE: SAP TM consultant in training

KNOWLEDGE TO ACQUIRE: Scope of logistics integration: TM can be integrated with components in the same system or an external system; orders from SD and MM, deliveries from LE and JIT calls from nJIT drive transportation planning and execution, and the TM component creates or updates freight units directly for documents defined in Customizing as TM-relevant when they are saved | What integration enables: collective planning for all orders and scheduling agreements, planning on sales or purchase orders before delivery creation for an earlier forecast of transportation demand, tendering, subcontracting and execution based on orders, sales order scheduling, and planning and execution of JIT calls | Integratable documents: orders (sales order, customer returns, purchase order, stock transport order, returns purchase order, returns stock transport order), scheduling agreements (sales, purchase, stock transport), JIT calls (sequenced, forwarded) and inbound and outbound deliveries from LE, also when an order was not sent to TM | Transportation requirements as sources: OTR is the demand from sales, purchasing and stock transport (SO, PO, STO); DTR is the demand from an inbound or outbound delivery (IDL/ODL); the freight unit represents the demand that is planned | Triggering documents and deployment models: sales orders, customer returns, purchase orders, stock transport orders and their returns, scheduling agreements and deliveries trigger transportation requirements; in side-by-side deployment the information is replicated and becomes order-based or delivery-based requirements via XML messages (SAP PI or point-to-point); in embedded deployment no separate document is needed and the freight unit is created directly from the original object | Decentralized scenarios: a decentralized EWM and TM system can be integrated with an external ERP system and transportation planning done on outbound delivery orders; settings sit under Decentralized EWM-TM Integration | Transportation relevance and control keys: control keys define which documents in a chain are integrated; sales document integration is activated by assigning a control key to sales document type, sales area and shipping condition; purchasing documents by document type, purchasing organization and purchasing group; delivery documents by shipping point, delivery type and shipping condition; the key is stored in parameter TM_CONTROL_KEY and passed to all relevant documents in the chain | Deliveries inherit the control key of the preceding document and ignore the delivery Customizing in that case; the delivery setting is needed only when a delivery is integrated but its order is not | Three settings named by S48600 for integrating sales orders, scheduling agreements and deliveries: activate integration via control key, create a logistics integration profile, assign the profile to the document type; prerequisite for configuration is the location creation report /SAPAPO/CREATE_LOCATION | Logistics integration profile for SD, MM and LE: common settings (freight unit building rule, condition for FUB rule determination, automatic freight unit building, stage building variant) and order-related settings (plan on requested or confirmed quantities, FU building for partly complete sales documents, rule group for the time range of purchase scheduling agreements, integration based on actual quantity, improved location determination, Advanced SR compliance check) | FUB rule determination: the system first checks the condition in the logistics integration profile, then the freight unit building rule in the profile, then standard settings; freight unit building can run automatically on save or be triggered manually or by background report (/SCMTMS/SUBSEQENT_FUB_LOGINT in embedded deployment) | Order integration process: order created in SD/MM with items and schedule lines, TM creates freight units only for transportation-relevant orders, planning can consolidate FUs from orders of different types (for example purchase and sales orders), then TM can execute transportation or trigger delivery creation in LE | End-to-end process from order to execution: create order (SD/MM), perform transportation planning (TM), create delivery proposal (TM), create deliveries (LE), reassign freight units from order to delivery (TM), execute transportation; the TM document flow can be displayed in SD, MM or LE | Delivery proposals: TM proposes inbound or outbound deliveries from planning results and LE creates them; creation runs by background report /SCMTMS/DLV_BATCH, interactively from the Delivery Proposal Builder app or Freight Orders - Worklist app, from the transportation cockpit or from the freight order UI; dates come from the freight order or booking, otherwise from FUs, quantities from the FUs | Delivery proposal consolidation and control: FUs can share a proposal only with the same source and destination location, Incoterms, shipping conditions, document type and (if planned) start and destination stop; TM respects chronological consumption of schedule lines in LE; the delivery profile (Create Delivery Profile app) controls fixing of planning results, grouping by freight unit, order or order item, and exclusion of planning or execution blocked objects | Delivery integration without order integration: if the order is not TM-relevant, TM creates new FUs for the delivery according to the specified FUB rule; changes to orders and deliveries update the FUs, a deleted order deletes its FUs, a deleted delivery reassigns FUs to the order if it is TM-relevant, otherwise deletes them | Stock transport orders: a two-step process with outbound and inbound deliveries and reassignment of FUs from outbound to inbound delivery; in the Advanced Shipping and Receiving scenario goods issue triggers the inbound delivery and the reassignment, and FUs in one delivery must belong to the same EWM- or LE-managed warehouse or storage location | Scheduling agreement integration: schedule lines of purchase scheduling agreements create, change or delete freight units; TM planning results can be synchronized back to MM schedule lines in batch only for FUs not yet assigned to a delivery and within the planning time fence; inbound deliveries from an ASN lead TM to reassign FUs from the agreement to the delivery | Integration time range for purchase scheduling agreements: start and end dates limit the schedule lines taken into account and so the freight units created; the start date is set once from the earliest schedule line with open quantity and a safety margin and is never in the future; the end date follows the schedule line count, matching rule and offsets and is determined daily; report /SCMTMS/UPDATE_INT_TIMERANGE preponed end dates | Order-only integration of purchase scheduling agreements: activation report /SCMTMS/ACTIVATE_MMSAG_WO_DLV sets a start date and a reduction quantity strategy, adjustable later with /SCMTMS/ADAPT_START_MM_WO_DLV | JIT call integration: FUs are created from sequenced JIT calls based on the component group material, with JIT call reference number, receiving point and unloading point;
